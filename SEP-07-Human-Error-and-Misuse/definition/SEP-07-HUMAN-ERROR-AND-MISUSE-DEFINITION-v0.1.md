## 1. Purpose

SEP-07 defines a bounded human-error-and-misuse safety layer.

The layer addresses cases in which upstream identity, authority, policy admissibility,
technical validity and authentic human approval may all be valid, while observable
properties of the requested action nevertheless conflict with a frozen stated human
objective or an explicit frozen human-use safety constraint.

The layer exists to preserve human authority without treating human approval as
automatic proof that an action is safe to execute.
