# ELIAS SAFETY EXPANSION PROGRAMME

## SEP-00 — CLAIM BOUNDARY DEFINITION v0.1

STATUS: PROSPECTIVE / NOT FROZEN
PROGRAMME: Elias Safety Expansion Programme (SEP)
OBJECT: SEP-00 Claim Boundary Freeze
VERSION: 0.1

---

## 1. PURPOSE

SEP exists to test whether the established Elias governance and execution-control architecture can be extended toward stronger, evidence-bounded safety properties.

SEP SHALL NOT begin from a presumption that Elias is generally safe.

SEP SHALL begin from the opposite evidentiary position:

BROADER CONSEQUENCE-SAFETY CLAIMS ARE NOT ESTABLISHED UNTIL PROSPECTIVELY TESTED.

The purpose of SEP is to locate the current safety boundary, attack that boundary, preserve failures and successes without retrospective repair, engineer only where evidence identifies a gap, and enlarge claims only where the resulting evidence warrants enlargement.

---

## 2. CURRENT ESTABLISHED CLAIM SURFACE

Prior Elias work has produced evidence within separately frozen scopes concerning properties including:

- identity handling;
- authority resolution;
- capability / authority separation;
- pre-execution admissibility;
- changed-condition reassessment;
- refusal under defined invalid or absent authority conditions;
- execution-boundary controls;
- evidence and receipt preservation;
- witness / audit continuity;
- bounded adversarial execution testing.

Those prior results remain governed by their own frozen definitions, evidence, limitations, and receipts.

SEP-00 DOES NOT enlarge those earlier claims.

SEP-00 DOES NOT retroactively reinterpret earlier results as proof of general AI safety.

---

## 3. CURRENT NON-CLAIMS

At SEP commencement, the following broader properties SHALL be treated as NOT ESTABLISHED unless and until separately tested:

- universal AI safety;
- general harm prevention;
- safety across arbitrary environments;
- safety across unknown future conditions;
- correctness of all authorised actions;
- correctness of all policies;
- complete hazard identification;
- complete consequence prediction;
- safe behaviour under every dependency failure;
- safe behaviour under every human error or misuse condition;
- safe behaviour under arbitrary system composition;
- guaranteed recovery from every harmful execution;
- indefinite safety under long-duration drift;
- safety under every adversarial strategy;
- whole-system safety.

Absence of evidence of failure SHALL NOT constitute evidence of safety.

---

## 4. SAFETY EXPANSION PRINCIPLE

A technically valid and correctly authorised action MAY still produce an unacceptable consequence.

Therefore:

IDENTITY VALID
+
AUTHORITY VALID
+
POLICY PERMISSION VALID
+
EXECUTION PATH VALID

DOES NOT BY ITSELF ESTABLISH:

CONSEQUENCE SAFE

SEP will test whether consequence-sensitive governance can be added without collapsing the existing identity, authority, admissibility, execution, and witness boundaries.

---

## 5. FIRST SAFETY QUESTION

The first prospective safety question is:

CAN ELIAS WITHHOLD A CORRECTLY AUTHORISED AND TECHNICALLY VALID ACTION SOLELY BECAUSE ITS EXPECTED CONSEQUENCE VIOLATES A FROZEN SAFETY CONSTRAINT?

At SEP-00 this proposition is:

NOT ESTABLISHED

No implementation result may be assumed in advance.

## 7. EVIDENTIARY RULES

SEP SHALL operate under the following rules:

1. Definitions precede implementation where a prospective test is being constituted.

2. Test conditions SHALL be preserved before result evaluation where practicable.

3. Failure SHALL remain failure.

4. NOT ESTABLISHED SHALL remain an admissible result.

5. Claims SHALL remain bounded to the tested object, conditions, environment, and evidence.

6. A passing narrow test SHALL NOT be converted into a universal safety claim.

7. Retrospective modification of a frozen test to obtain a preferred result is prohibited.

8. Changed definitions require a new version or successor object.

9. Material environmental change requires reassessment where the claim depends upon that environment.

10. Evidence integrity and execution integrity are separate from consequence safety and SHALL NOT be collapsed.

11. Independent reproduction or adversarial examination SHALL be distinguished from self-testing.

---

## 8. EXPANSION RULE

A safety claim may expand only when:

- the target property is explicitly defined;
- foreseeable failure modes are identified;
- prospective tests are constituted;
- evidence is preserved;
- contradictory evidence is retained;
- limitations remain explicit;
- the resulting claim is no broader than the evidence permits.

The programme SHALL prefer a narrow established claim over a broad unsupported claim.

## 9. INITIAL ARCHITECTURAL HYPOTHESIS

Existing simplified Elias execution flow:

IDENTITY
→ AUTHORITY
→ POLICY
→ ADMISSIBILITY
→ EXECUTION
→ WITNESS

SEP will investigate whether stronger safety assurance requires additional governed stages such as:

PURPOSE
→ IDENTITY
→ AUTHORITY
→ CONTEXT
→ POLICY
→ HAZARD / CONSEQUENCE ASSESSMENT
→ ADMISSIBILITY
→ EXECUTION
→ OUTCOME VERIFICATION
→ CONTAINMENT / RECOVERY
→ WITNESS
→ REASSESSMENT

This is an architectural hypothesis only.

It is NOT an established architecture at SEP-00.
