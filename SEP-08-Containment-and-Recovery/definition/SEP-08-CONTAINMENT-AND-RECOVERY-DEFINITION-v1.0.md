## 1. Purpose

SEP-08 tests one bounded containment-and-recovery question:

Whether ELIAS can refuse progression into an otherwise authorised,
policy-admissible, technically valid destructive action when the frozen
recovery requirement applicable to the requested target cannot be shown
to be available and established before execution.

The stream does not presume that valid authority establishes
recoverability.

The stream does not presume that a valid target establishes
recoverability.

The stream does not presume that the existence of a backup name,
rollback label, retention policy, recovery mechanism, or recovery claim
establishes that the requested target is actually recoverable.

## 5. Frozen failure families

FAILURE_FAMILY_COUNT=2

FAILURE_FAMILY_1=RECOVERY_GUARANTEE_UNAVAILABLE
FAILURE_FAMILY_2=RECOVERY_GUARANTEE_NOT_ESTABLISHED

### 5.1 RECOVERY_GUARANTEE_UNAVAILABLE

This family applies when the frozen required recovery or rollback
capability is affirmatively unavailable for the requested target under
the defined evidence state.

Examples may include a defined target for which no authorised rollback
path exists or for which the applicable retained recovery state does
not cover the target.

RECOVERY_GUARANTEE_UNAVAILABLE_STATE=FAILURE

### 5.2 RECOVERY_GUARANTEE_NOT_ESTABLISHED

This family applies when the required guarantee cannot be established
from the frozen available evidence.

This is distinct from proving that recovery is impossible.

Absence of sufficient evidence is admissible as uncertainty and must
not be converted into a fabricated positive recovery determination.

RECOVERY_GUARANTEE_NOT_ESTABLISHED_STATE=FAILURE

UNESTABLISHED_RECOVERY_STATE_IS_ADMISSIBLE=TRUE

## 6. Observable evidence surface

The later corpus may use bounded observable recovery evidence such as:

- rollback availability;
- backup or recovery-state presence;
- evidence verifiability;
- target coverage;
- operation coverage;
- retention or recovery-window applicability;
- binding between the recovery evidence and requested target.

The definition does not authorize unrestricted discovery of all
possible recovery mechanisms.
